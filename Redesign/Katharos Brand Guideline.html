01

The name

καθαρός — katharós — is the Greek word for pure, clean, clear. It is the oldest possible promise to make about a landscape: that it can be returned to itself. The brand carries that promise lightly. Not a campaign against dirt, but an invitation toward clarity.

Where the old identity shouted in bright blue and green, Katharos speaks in a calmer register — Aegean water, olive groves, marble and sand. Civic technology that feels less like a complaint form and more like an act of care.

02

The mark

An olive leaf-droplet rising from Aegean waves, held in a thin circle. Leaf for nature, drop for water and place, the ring for wholeness. A single sky-blue point marks the spot.

Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space of at least half the emblem's height on every side. Minimum legible size: 24px (digital), 9mm (print). Never recolour the leaf away from olive or the dot away from sky.

App icon & map markers

Map markers keep the leaf-drop DNA as a filled teardrop, tinted by how long a report has waited — olive (fresh) → sand → amber → terracotta (long ignored).

We sound like

Calm, warm, certain. The citizen is a capable participant, not a "user". Imperatives are invitations: Photograph. Submit. Track. Sentence case; wide-tracked uppercase only for small labels. Greek is canonical.

We avoid

Alarm, blame, bureaucracy, exclamation pile-ups, emoji as decoration. We never insult a municipality — accountability copy is pointed but factual: which places act, and which don't yet.
